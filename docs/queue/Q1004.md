# The carried rows were moved, not groomed

Ten rows arrived with the skills they target. Their targets and exhibit
citations were re-pointed and the private skill names they carried were
inverted to roles, because both of those break or leak. Their **claims** were
left exactly as filed, which was the right call at the time — re-deriving ten
findings during a move is how a move turns into a rewrite — and is now a debt.

**The ceiling arithmetic is done.** [Q292](Q292.md), [Q300](Q300.md) and
[Q301](Q301.md) each closed by pricing an addition against a `BODY_BUDGET`
figure, a per-skill dict of byte counts that residency tiers replaced. All three
were re-measured and restated on 2026-09-14: they now defer the body figures to
[Q1003](Q1003.md) and say what the retired mechanism actually changed, which is
that a body held shrink-only has no budget to raise, so displacement is the only
way an addition lands. [Q274](Q274.md) also names `BODY_BUDGET` and was left
alone deliberately — there it is the *subject* of a recorded 2026-09-03
measurement rather than a live price, and re-pointing it would misreport what
was measured.

**The other five carried rows' claims have still not been re-derived.**
[Q146](Q146.md), [Q234](Q234.md), [Q259](Q259.md), [Q274](Q274.md) and
[Q298](Q298.md) assert mechanisms and sizes that nobody has checked against this
tree. Their exhibit citations all resolve exactly — `queue.py lint
--citation-window 0` was clean over the whole store on 2026-09-14 — so what is
unchecked is the reasoning around the citations, not the pointers.

**Five bare pull-request numbers name a repository this store never
identifies**: `#361` in Q259, `#444`, `#445` and `#446`
in Q274, and `#472` in Q292. They are pull requests in the private collection these rows were filed under. A
reader here cannot resolve any of them, and a bare `#N` in a repo with a remote
would auto-link to the wrong thing entirely. Decide per hit whether the number
is load-bearing provenance that should name its repository, or colour that can
be cut.
