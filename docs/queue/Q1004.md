# The ten carried rows were moved, not groomed

Ten rows arrived with the skills they target. Their targets and exhibit
citations were re-pointed and the private skill names they carried were
inverted to roles, because both of those break or leak. Their **claims** were
left exactly as filed, which was the right call at the time — re-deriving ten
findings during a move is how a move turns into a rewrite — and is now a debt.

**At least three assert a size against a mechanism that no longer exists.**
[Q292](Q292.md), [Q300](Q300.md) and [Q301](Q301.md) each say a body is
"exactly at its ceiling" against a `BODY_BUDGET` figure. `BODY_BUDGET` was a
per-skill dict of byte counts and was replaced by residency tiers; the number
each row quotes matches nothing the gate now reads. A session picking one of
those rows meets a precise figure that cannot be reproduced.

**The rows are not wrong about their findings**, which is what makes this easy
to skip. Each one's actual subject — an agreeing figure read as corroboration, a
control taken in a second process, a selectivity rate read as throughput — is
unaffected. Only the placement arithmetic at the end of each row is stale.

**What a groom has to do:** re-measure each body against its current tier
ceiling and grandfathered baseline, and either restate the displacement cost or
strike the paragraph. Also re-read the rows for the neighbour references that
now point at items outside this store, which the store README lists.

Filed at the extraction, 2026-09-13, by the session that moved them.
