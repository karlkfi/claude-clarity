# Rewrite tech-docs-layers for the repo that has no docs conventions

[`decisions.md`](../decisions.md) settles that this skill lost a competition
rather than never entering one: in `github-actions-gateway`, which holds 433 of
the 654 measured docs occasions and is the only checkout here with a docs
framework, the loaded `CLAUDE.md` reaches this skill's moment three times and
routes all three to two local pages. The skill is named only in that repo's
`docs/development/skills.md`, which nothing loads.

The case it has never been scoped to is the other one: **a repo with a `docs/`
tree and no docs conventions of its own**. Four of the six checkouts here are
that — `claude-bouncer`, `claude-pr-sentinel`, `claude-spill-guard` and
`claude-ground-truth` carry no `documentation-standards.md`, against
`github-actions-gateway` and `kube-headroom` which do. Those four hold 221 of
the 654 occasion sessions.

## Measured 2026-09-24

Body split, by bytes over 11,725:

| part | bytes | share | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Step 1 survey | 1,314 | 11.2% | behaviour, not knowledge |
| Layers 1–2 terminology, cross-refs | 1,632 | 13.9% | applies without a framework |
| Layers 3–6 version, metadata, nav, partials | 3,352 | 28.6% | **each presupposes a framework** |
| Step 3 sweep | 1,983 | 16.9% | behaviour, not knowledge |

Framework presence, read from the checkouts: `github-actions-gateway` has
`mkdocs.yml` over 428 pages. `claude-bouncer` (67 pages), `claude-spill-guard`
(81), `claude-pr-sentinel` (54), `kube-headroom` (22) and `claude-ground-truth`
(5) have none. So layers 3 to 6 are inapplicable in every repo except the one
that already declined the skill.

## Two candidate shapes

**Narrow it.** Cut layers 3–6, keep terminology, cross-references, the survey
and the sweep. Roughly 3 kB against 11.7 kB, aimed at the 221 sessions the
current body overshoots. Cheapest, and it leaves the skill answering the same
question with less.

**Flip it to bootstrapping.** What worked in the gateway was not applying six
layers to a page — it was writing that repo its own `doc-update-matrix.md` and
`documentation-standards.md`, then promoting the sweep into six gates
(`doc-links`, `doc-toc-check`, `roadmap-check`, `script-docs-check`,
`release-links-check`, `verify-published-docs`). A skill whose moment is *this
repo has no docs conventions doc* would produce the local page rather than
compete with it, and would be doing what the one successful case did. It is
closer to a rename than an edit.

## What is held rather than checked

**The six layers' content is ordinary technical-writing knowledge a model
already carries, and what the body adds is the instruction to survey first and
sweep after** — 28.1% of it. That is a claim about training data and nothing
here can settle it. It is the load-bearing assumption under both shapes, so a
rewrite that keeps the layer exposition is betting against it.

Cheapest available test short of measuring: run the same doc task twice against
a framework-less repo, once with the skill loaded and once without, and read
whether the difference is sweep behaviour or layer vocabulary.

**Ask:** which shape, and whether the retirement question reopens if neither
lands. The narrow version competes with nothing and may still fire zero times,
because the finding above is that the description is not the lever.
