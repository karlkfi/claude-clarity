# Move the low-uptake rules of `verify-claims` behind `references/`

The route to room in a shrink-only body is settled and the move is not made.
[`decisions.md`](../decisions.md) records why relocation is the only route —
the tier ladder has no band above `hot`, so the reassignment
[`skill-body-ceilings.md`](../skill-body-ceilings.md) names as the reversal
cannot fire for this body. This row is the move itself.

**What moves.** The 21 rules the residency reading puts at `BLIND` or `FLAT`,
21,120 bytes of the 110,854-byte body, re-measured 2026-09-19 at `6b88023`
under the two-flag invocation. The reading and the invocation are in
[`verify-claims-residency.md`](../verify-claims-residency.md); take it again
rather than quoting these bytes, because the corpus grows.

**What the reading licenses.** A ranking for relocation, and not a verdict that
anything is unused. Fourteen of the 21 are `BLIND` — the probe never saw their
markers anywhere, which the instrument's own docstring calls a fact about the
probe. Relocation is reversible and ships the rule either way, so a ranking is
enough to decide what leaves the hot path; deletion would need a verdict the
reading cannot give, and nothing here may delete a rule.

**Three rows wait on this and each costs about a kilobyte.** Q274, Q1010 and
Q1012 park measured clauses that were written and reverted for want of space.
They are not the deliverable — the move is — but they are what the room is for,
and whether the three fold into one clause is argued in Q1010 and still open.

**Why it did not land with the decision.** Every one of the 21 rules sits above
`skills/verify-claims/SKILL.md:1343`, so any deletion shifts the three
`path:N:text` citations in Q292, Q298 and Q301. A concurrent lane was
repointing those same three files from the other side, and a citation two open
branches both move resolves to a number that is wrong whichever lands second.
Re-read that state before starting; it was true on 2026-09-19 and is the kind
of fact that expires.

**What would reverse it:** a reading that separates consulted from applied. The
present one cannot see a rule applied in the reader's own words, so it ranks by
what reaches a session's output rather than by what a reader needed. If Claude
Code ever reports which part of a resident body a turn attended to, the
candidate set is re-derived before anything moves.
