# One body is 4.5 times its ceiling and may only shrink

Measured 2026-09-13: `verify-claims`'s body is **109,816 bytes** against the
24 KiB hot-tier ceiling, and it is 65% of the bundle by lines including its
three `references/` files. Every other skill here is between 8.4 KB and 18 KB.

It is grandfathered, so the gate is not red — the limit is the looser of the
tier ceiling and the size measured at the merge base, which asserts only that no
body grew on this branch. That is the shape working as designed and it is also
why nothing reports the gap.

**The cost is per call, not per file.** A body is resident for the rest of the
session that loaded it, so it is charged its size times the calls that follow.
At the hot tier that is roughly a hundred times per session, and this one is
paid at 4.5× what the tier assumes.

**The split is already the plan and already started.** `references/` holds
`shell-traps.md`, `status-pages.md` and `timing-and-concurrency.md`, read on
demand rather than loaded with the body. What has not been decided is which of
the remaining sections are reference material and which are the rules a reader
needs in hand — and the skill's own framing makes that hard, since most of its
length is worked cases and a case is what makes a rule land.

**What would settle it:** a reading of which sections are consulted versus
applied. Section-level residency is not something the existing instruments
measure, so this needs an instrument before it needs an edit.

**Do not treat the ceiling as the target.** Shrinking to 24 KiB would delete
most of the worked cases, which is the content the skill is valued for. The
question is where the cases live, not whether they exist.

Filed at the extraction, 2026-09-13.
