# An expected red is the one result a spurious failure flatters

Blocked by [Q1014](Q1014.md). This row parks a clause for a body held
shrink-only, so it cannot land alone. Q1003 settled the route as relocation, and
this row's clause lands **in Q1014's own branch** rather than after it: room is
made and spent inside one branch, because the grandfather baseline is re-read per
branch, so a shrink that merges makes main's smaller size the new limit and banks
nothing. Measured in [`decisions.md`](../decisions.md).

**The clause.** The body already carries the green half —
`skills/verify-claims/SKILL.md:1064:**Repeated passes do not validate a flake fix.** A green run of twenty is equally consistent with`
— which says to invert the fix and confirm the suite fails, and stops there. One
red does not settle an inversion either, and it fails worse, because of an
asymmetry no other rule in the skill names: **an expected red is the one result
where a spurious failure reads as success.** Every other verification has the
tester wanting the opposite of what a broken instrument produces, so scepticism
has something to push against. An inversion reverses that — you broke the code on
purpose and you want the red, so a flake, an unrelated failure, or a subject that
is only intermittently vacuous all deliver exactly the observation you were
hoping for, and nothing in the method notices.

The operational half, and the reason to spend bytes on this rather than on
another description of a defect: **sample until the distribution stops moving,
and report the ratio rather than the verdict.** Argue against a fixed count. What
makes one sample insufficient is a property of the subject rather than a constant
— concurrency, timing, ordering, or a claim of intermittency in the thing being
checked — so the rule names when a single red stops settling anything instead of
naming a number. And say what a split distribution means, because the intuitive
reading is backwards: 9 FAIL / 1 PASS **confirms** the claim under test, it does
not refute it. A defect that reproduces one run in ten is a defect.

**The measurement, which is relayed.** Taken by neither the filing session nor
anything this repo can re-run: the subject is another repository's integration
suite, and nothing here samples it. Treat the figures as a report, not a reading.

From a four-PR `session-orchestrator` run on `github-actions-gateway`,
2026-09-24, run `nostalgic-nightingale-8f48c8`. A worker was dispatched on that
repo's Q541, a row asserting that
`TestV2_EgressProxy_CiliumFQDNCarriesReferrerGHESHost`, in
`cmd/gmc/internal/controller/integration/v2_egressproxy_fqdn_test.go`, passes
even with the watch it claims to exercise deleted. The worker verified the
causation claim the right way — deleted the mechanism — ran the mutant **once**,
got a red, and concluded the defect was not live. That conclusion reached the
orchestrator and the user twice. An independent reviewer ran the same mutation
and got a **green**. Six more samples followed. Final: **10 runs, 9 FAIL, 1
PASS**, with the mutation verified present in the tree and the package confirmed
to compile that source, so the green is a real observation rather than a mutation
that failed to land. The defect is real and intermittent; the row survived and is
still at the head of that repo's queue.

**What makes it a gap rather than a lapse: the worker satisfied every rule the
body carries.** It deleted one mechanism rather than the branch around it
(`skills/verify-claims/SKILL.md:952:- Delete one mechanism, not the branch around it. A deletion coarse enough to redden every`),
and it confirmed the mutation by reading the tree rather than by an exit status
(`skills/verify-claims/SKILL.md:1107:**A mutation is confirmed by reading the file it mutated, not by the exit status of the`).
Both held. The single sample is the one thing nothing asked about.

**Where it lands, and the decision.** Folded onto the flake-fix bullet in §3
(`skills/verify-claims/SKILL.md:946:## 3. Writing a check that can fail`), as the
red half of one two-halved rule — not as a generalisation of the near-miss at
`skills/verify-claims/SKILL.md:612:It misleads in two directions at once. The result is red, so *could this have shown me the`.
Both were read before deciding. Three reasons, in the order they decided it:

- **The defect is a missing sample count in a procedure the skill prescribes.** A
  reader following *invert the fix and confirm the suite fails* is the one who
  acts wrongly, and that reader is in §3. The near-miss is in §1, where the
  subject is reading a result you did not produce.
- **The two cases differ in what the author wanted.** At 612 the red is unwanted
  and the alignment is incidental — the paragraph observes that a spurious red
  survives *could this have shown me the opposite?*. In an inversion the red is
  the hoped-for result, which is the stronger form and the one that needs a
  procedure attached.
- **Generalising 612 would cost it its subject.** Its tell is specific and
  findable — a syntax or import error arriving inside a test assertion rather
  than as a collection error — and lifting the alignment out of that paragraph to
  state it abstractly leaves the mid-run-write case without its diagnosis.

So the asymmetry is stated once, where the inversion loop is prescribed, and 612
is left alone. No cross-reference either way: a reader in §1 debugging a
spurious red is not the reader about to run an inversion, so the pointer would be
bytes for nobody.

**Why it is parked, with both constraint numbers re-measured 2026-09-26 at
`f64bf46`.** `skills/verify-claims/SKILL.md` is 110,838 bytes, and
`verify-claims` is `hot` in `validate-skills.py`'s `SKILL_TIER` against a 24 KiB
hot ceiling — so the tier ladder is inert here exactly as Q1014 says, and
`oversize()` holds the body to `max(ceiling, baseline)` with the baseline at
110,838 on this branch's merge base. Any addition fails until the diff names what
it takes back out.

**Weighing the displacement, which this row does not settle by building one.** The
clause as drafted here measures 656 bytes; with the re-cut the existing bullet
needs to read as one rule with two halves, budget under 800. Against that: Q1014
establishes that every unit in this body cuttable without losing a claim is a
relocation candidate, and that compression over high-uptake prose returned 100,

leaving the file. Two things this row did **not** establish, and both bear on the

exchange rate is inherited rather than re-measured here; and the residency
