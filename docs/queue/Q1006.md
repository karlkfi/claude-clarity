# Three linters were ruled out and the ruling is untested

Two of the ten passes ship a linter. Three more were considered and refused on
2026-09-12, and the refusal is an argument rather than a measurement: all three
would emit warnings a human has to adjudicate, and that tier measurably goes
unread — three bodies sat past their ceilings across six commits and six gate
runs with the number raised zero times.

The three, and why each was refused:

- `semantic-remediation` is twelve semantic categories.
- `claim-provenance`'s scoring, and its choice among four repairs, are
  judgement.
- `tech-docs-layers` is about where docs live, so its checkable part is a
  doc-tree probe rather than a prose linter.

**The stated reversal condition is narrow and checkable**: one of the three
finding a rule that is *certain* and has a *mechanical repair*. That pair is
what made `readability`'s linter worth building — it covers the one rule that
skill's own body calls checkable, and refuses the two that need to know what the
reader already has.

**Measure:** nobody has gone looking. The condition was written the day the
ruling was made and no pass over the three bodies has been taken against it
since. This row is that pass: read each of the three for a rule where the script would be
sure *and* the fix is one edit it can name, and record what it found even when
the answer is none — a re-tested ruling that comes back the same is worth more
than an untested one.

**Do not lower the bar to find something.** A linter that emits an adjudicable
warning is the failure this ruling exists to prevent, and shipping one to close
a row would be the row defeating itself.
