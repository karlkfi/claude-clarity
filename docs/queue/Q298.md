# A measurement anchored to a wall time does not survive a wrong clock

`skills/verify-claims/SKILL.md:1343:A figure lifted from ambient context was never read from an instrument`
owns the live half of this: read the clock when you take the reading, and where
the receiver only needs the reading, send it undated. That governs a message,
which is consumed once. It says nothing about the figure that gets **committed**
— into a backlog row, a PR body, a commit message, a comment in a script — and
outlives every session that could re-derive it.

**The constructive rule is the missing one.** A measurement anchored to a
revision survives a wrong clock: `read at 8adb878` or `measured 2026-09-07` is
re-derivable by anyone, from the tree, whatever the clock that produced it was
doing. One anchored to a wall time is checkable against nothing once the session
is gone — and a timezone suffix makes it read as taken rather than guessed,
which is the property that makes the wrong ones survive review.

## Measured 2026-09-07 on `karlkfi/claude-spill-guard`

Reported by that run: its orchestrator fabricated timestamps in peer messages,
extrapolating rather than running `date`, drifting up to 80 minutes, with
timezones attached. The drift figure is the run's own account and is not
re-derived here; the durable half is, and it came back clean. A worker audited
what it had committed — its four filed rows, its merged diff's added lines, and
both PR bodies — for `HH:MM`, `PDT` and `PST`, and found zero hits, because
every claim it committed was anchored to a SHA or a calendar day.

**That audit is also the instrument**, which is what makes this a rule with a
check rather than an exhortation: grep what a branch adds for a clock time, and
each hit is either a citation of a record with its own timestamp or a claim
nobody can re-take.

## Direction

One paragraph beside the ambient-figure rule, stating the anchor choice for a
durable artifact and naming the grep. Keep it distinct from this repository's
own *Date a measurement in host-local time* convention, which fixes the
**timezone** of a date going into the tree and takes for granted that a date is
what is written; this rule is about choosing the anchor in the first place, and
it holds in any repository.
